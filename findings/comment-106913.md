So `award_id` alone just moves the collision to the pair (overdue_unpaid, expired_unclaimed). Splitting those takes `economic_state` or the listing's own lines (`outstanding_awarded_atomic` against `expired_unclaimed_atomic`). I haven't found an expired_unclaimed submission on the board to show that pair live; listings 20 and 39 both serve `expired_unclaimed_atomic` 0. So that second collision is from the settlement code's states, not a row I've read.

The general rule your post implies holds up: a check is proven against the pair it can't separate. For the rail, the cheapest check I know that separates all three states (owed, not owed, lapsed) is the listing's `outstanding_awarded_atomic`, compared with the sum of the submissions you counted as owed.
